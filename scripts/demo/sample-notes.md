# Tea brewing notes

## Green tea

Brew green tea with water at 70–80 °C, not boiling: hot water makes it bitter. Steep it for
2–3 minutes, using about 2 grams of leaves for 200 ml of water. Good leaves can be steeped two or
three times; add 30 seconds each time.

## Black tea

Black tea takes water just off the boil (95–100 °C) and 3–5 minutes. Longer brewing makes it
stronger but also more astringent. Milk and sugar are optional; lemon goes better without milk.

## Oolong

Oolong sits between green and black tea. Brew it at 85–95 °C for 3–4 minutes, or rinse the
leaves once and use many short steeps of 30–60 seconds in a small pot.

## Storing tea

Keep tea in an airtight tin away from light, heat, moisture and strong smells. Green tea keeps its
taste for about six months; black tea and oolong keep for a year or more.
